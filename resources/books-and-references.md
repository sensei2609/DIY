## Mathematics & Physics

### Essential Mathematics

**📘 "Calculus" by James Stewart**
- Comprehensive, clear explanations
- Focus on Chapters 3-5 (derivatives), 6-7 (integrals), 15-16 (multivariable)
- Best for: Foundational calculus concepts
- Level: Undergraduate

**📘 "Linear Algebra and Its Applications" by Gilbert Strang**
- Gold standard for linear algebra
- MIT OCW companion videos available
- Essential for: Rotation matrices, state-space control
- Level: Undergraduate

**📘 "Advanced Engineering Mathematics" by Erwin Kreyszig**
- All math you need: ODEs, PDEs, Fourier, complex analysis
- Best for: Reference, graduate level
- Chapters 1-6, 10-11 most relevant
- Level: Advanced undergraduate / Graduate

**📗 FREE: "The Scientist and Engineer's Guide to Digital Signal Processing" by Steven W. Smith**
- Available free online: www.dspguide.com
- Excellent for FFT, filters, convolution
- Very practical approach
- Level: All levels

### Physics

**📘 "University Physics" by Young & Freedman**
- Comprehensive physics textbook
- Focus: Mechanics (Ch 1-14), Electricity & Magnetism (Ch 21-32)
- Best for: Solid foundation
- Level: Undergraduate

**📘 "Introduction to Electrodynamics" by David J. Griffiths**
- Essential for radar engineering
- Maxwell's equations, EM waves
- Mathematical but clear
- Level: Advanced undergraduate

**📘 "Classical Mechanics" by John R. Taylor**
- Lagrangian/Hamiltonian mechanics
- Relevant for: Advanced dynamics, control
- Level: Advanced undergraduate

## Control Systems

### Core Texts

**📘 "Modern Control Engineering" by Katsuhiko Ogata**
- Industry standard
- PID, state-space, frequency response
- MATLAB examples (adaptable to Python)
- Level: Undergraduate / Professional

**📘 "Feedback Control of Dynamic Systems" by Franklin, Powell, Emami-Naeini**
- Excellent for practical implementation
- Digital control, state estimation
- Real-world examples
- Level: Undergraduate / Professional

**📗 "PID Controllers: Theory, Design, and Tuning" by Åström and Hägglund**
- Definitive guide to PID
- Tuning methods, practical insights
- Best for: Implementation
- Level: Professional

### Advanced Control

**📕 "Optimal State Estimation" by Dan Simon**
- Kalman filters, particle filters
- Clear explanations with examples
- Essential for: Sensor fusion
- Level: Graduate

**📕 "Nonlinear Dynamics and Chaos" by Steven Strogatz**
- Understanding complex systems
- Relevant for: Stability analysis
- Beautiful explanations
- Level: Advanced undergraduate

## Electronics & Circuit Design

### Fundamentals

**📘 "The Art of Electronics" by Horowitz & Hill**
- THE electronics bible
- Practical, intuitive approach
- Every engineer should own this
- Level: All levels

**📘 "Practical Electronics for Inventors" by Paul Scherz & Simon Monk**
- Very accessible, hands-on
- Great for beginners
- Covers basics to microcontrollers
- Level: Beginner / Intermediate

**📘 "Electronic Principles" by Albert Malvino & David Bates**
- Solid fundamentals
- Good problem sets
- Level: Undergraduate

### Power Electronics

**📕 "Fundamentals of Power Electronics" by Erickson & Maksimović**
- Buck/boost converters, PWM
- Essential for: Battery management, motor drives
- Level: Advanced undergraduate

**📗 "Switching Power Supply Design" by Abraham I. Pressman**
- Practical design guide
- Real circuits, components selection
- Level: Professional

### PCB Design

**📗 "High-Speed Digital Design: A Handbook of Black Magic" by Johnson & Graham**
- Signal integrity, grounding
- Essential for: RF, high-speed designs
- Level: Professional

**📗 "Printed Circuit Board Design Techniques for EMC Compliance" by Mark Montrose**
- EMI/EMC considerations
- Layout best practices
- Level: Professional

## Academic Papers

### Quadcopter Control

**Must-Read Papers:**

1. **"Full control of a quadrotor"** by Pounds et al. (2010)
   - Fundamental control architecture
   - Cascaded PID loops

2. **"Feedback Linearization for Quadrotors"** by Hoffmann et al. (2007)
   - Advanced control techniques
   - Aggressive maneuvers

3. **"Design of a Four Rotor Aerial Robot"** by Bouabdallah et al. (2004)
   - Complete system design
   - Practical insights

4. **"The Role of Propeller Aerodynamics in Quadcopter Control"** by Powers et al. (2015)
   - Propeller effects on control
   - Moment scaling

### Radar Signal Processing

**Must-Read Papers:**

1. **"FMCW Radar Range-Doppler Processing"** by Meta et al. (2007)
   - 2D FFT processing
   - Implementation details

2. **"Automotive Radar Signal Processing"** by Rohling & Meinecke (2001)
   - CFAR detection
   - Clutter rejection

3. **"Micro-Doppler Signatures"** by Chen et al. (2006)
   - Target classification
   - Feature extraction

4. **"Short-Range FMCW Radar Design"** by Pohl et al. (2010)
   - 24 GHz radar implementation
   - Practical considerations

### Access Papers

**Resources:**
- IEEE Xplore (university access or individual subscription)
- arXiv.org (free pre-prints)
- ResearchGate (author uploads)
- Google Scholar (find free PDFs)

## Certifications & Regulations

### Drone Regulations (USA)

**FAA Part 107**
- Required for commercial drone operation
- Written exam
- Study Guide: FAA "Remote Pilot - Small Unmanned Aircraft Systems Study Guide"

**Resources:**
- FAA DroneZone (register drones)
- B4UFLY app (check airspace)
- AirMap (flight planning)

### Amateur Radio (for RF experimentation)

**Ham Radio License**
- Technician class - Entry level, HF access
- General class - More frequencies
- Extra class - All privileges

**Study Resources:**
- ARRL License Manual
- HamStudy.org (free practice exams)
- HamTestOnline (paid, interactive)

**Why get licensed?**
- Legal access to RF spectrum for experimentation
- Community support
- Emergency communications

### RF Emissions Compliance

**FCC (USA):**
- Part 15 - Unlicensed devices
- Keep power low (<10mW for 24 GHz)

**CE (Europe):**
- RED Directive
- Similar power limits

**Note:** DIY radar typically falls under Part 15. Stay under power limits!

## Learning Pathways by Topic

### Path 1: Quadcopter Builder

1. **Start:** "Practical Electronics for Inventors"
2. **Control:** "Modern Control Engineering" (Ogata)
3. **Embedded:** "Making Embedded Systems" (White)
4. **Drone-specific:** "Small Unmanned Aircraft" (Beard & McLain)
5. **Build:** Follow build guides + YouTube tutorials
6. **Advanced:** "Quadcopter Dynamics" (Quan)

**Timeline:** 6-12 months to first flight, 2+ years to mastery

### Path 2: Radar Engineer

1. **Start:** "The Art of Electronics"
2. **RF Basics:** ARRL Handbook
3. **Math:** DSP Guide (free online)
4. **Radar:** "Introduction to Radar Systems" (Skolnik)
5. **Practical:** "Small and Short-Range Radar" (Charvat)
6. **Build:** Start with Doppler module, progress to FMCW
7. **Advanced:** Academic papers on specific topics

**Timeline:** 1+ year to working system, 3+ years to expertise

### Path 3: Control Engineer

1. **Start:** Linear algebra (Strang), Calculus (Stewart)
2. **Core:** "Feedback Control" (Franklin et al.)
3. **Implementation:** "Modern Control Engineering" (Ogata)
4. **Estimation:** "Optimal State Estimation" (Simon)
5. **Practice:** Implement on hardware (Arduino + motor)
6. **Advanced:** "Nonlinear Dynamics" (Strogatz)

**Timeline:** 2-3 years for solid foundation
